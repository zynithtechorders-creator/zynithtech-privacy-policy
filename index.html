Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Purpose

ZynithTech Growth Tools is a private tool intended to help its owner analyze business performance, sales information, and advertising campaign reports for accounts they are authorized to access.

The tool may process order information, product and inventory details, advertising campaign metrics, and reports that the account owner retrieves from Amazon services or imports manually.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to review business performance, generate analytics, and support business decisions. Amazon services may receive requests when the tool accesses their APIs.

Storage and Sharing

Application credentials are intended to be stored in server-side environment configuration, not in the browser or public source files. Imported reports and application data may be stored locally by the owner. Information is not intentionally sold or shared with unrelated third parties.

Retention and Security

Locally stored information remains until the owner deletes it. Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and business data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Use of Amazon services is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. ZynithTech Growth Tools is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner using the contact details associated with the application.
